A letter from Wren's Hollow, and a game to go with it

Thank you for buying one of our games. This is a second, complete game, free, with a letter from the constable of Wren's Hollow on the front of it.

It is not a sample or a teaser. The Wet Half-Hour is a whole four-player murder mystery: four suspect cards, eleven printed case documents, a guest sheet, a host sheet, accusation slips and a sheet for the table to fill in together. It takes about forty-five minutes and roughly ten minutes to print and cut. Nothing is memorised, nothing is read aloud in character, nobody lies, and nobody knows they are the culprit — including the culprit.

What is in the file

  • Page 1 — a letter from Constable Dorothea Pruett, on her own notepaper.
  • Pages 2–22 — the whole game, in the order you print it.

The answer is not in that file. It lives in a second file, behind a full-page STOP card, so whoever does the printing can sit down and play like everybody else.

Printing it

  • Print at 100% “Actual size”, not “fit to page”. Both files are laid out to fall inside US Letter and A4 without scaling.
  • Two pages hold the four suspect cards, two to a page, with a dashed “cut here” line across the middle. Cut those into four.
  • Everything prints legibly in black and white on ordinary paper. Nothing is set below 11 point.
  • Print the answer file last, and do not read it.

Playing it with three, or five

At three, leave out Dr Quill's card and read it aloud as a written statement instead — everything on it also appears on one of the documents. At five, the fifth person keeps the time, collects the accusation slips and reads out the answer at the end, which is the best job at the table.
